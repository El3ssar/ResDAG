    Vanilla forecasting robustness¶

    The previous independent-trajectory study found no prediction regression against the accepted implementation. It also found that the friendly vanilla recipes fell short of the typical seven-to-eight Lyapunov-time goal. This continuation tests stronger recipes without choosing settings on those inspected test runs.

    What changes in the model¶

    rd.ESN(..., input_to_readout=False) fits the prediction head on reservoir memory alone. The default True also supplies the current input. The same setting is available on classic_esn; ordinary construction keeps its default. Automatic vanilla tuning searches both choices with its existing budgets. OTT's augmented features are unchanged. See the model workflow for usage and the tuning reference for controls.

    Changing this switch does not change initialized reservoir weights. The first two trials below compare identical settings and reservoir weights, differing only in the head's features. Subsequent trials vary several settings, so their results describe whole recipes rather than the effect of this switch alone.

    Validation before final testing¶

    The checked-in runner is benchmarks/vanilla_robustness.py. It uses standard Optuna TPE and public Torch fit/forecast calls. Each candidate is fitted separately on three new trajectories per system, with three reservoir seeds and three nonoverlapping forecast starting points. A batch fit would share one readout across trajectories and would answer a different question.

    Both systems use canonical Lorenz/Rössler parameters and TSDynamics DOP853 integration at rtol=1e-10, atol=1e-12. The protocol records exact initial conditions, sampling intervals and solver settings before integration. The search attempts at most 24 configurations per system with 600 seconds of soft search time. An admitted configuration finishes all its seed/origin checks; neither pruning nor optional screening is enabled. Both controlled baseline trials must complete, even if the time budget expires before additional trials.

    Search controls are width, spectral radius, leak, input strength, bias strength, recurrent degree, ridge strength and the input-to-readout choice. The numerical policy is fixed: float64 Torch SVD with an unpenalized intercept. Training-only standard scaling and full observed history are used throughout.

    The objective is the median of the three trajectory medians at the 0.3 error threshold. Each trajectory median contains nine seed/origin forecasts. Per-step error is RMS error divided componentwise by training standard deviation, measured on raw[washout + 1 : train_observations], matching the preceding primary protocol. A forecast ends at its first error greater than or equal to the threshold, or its first nonfinite error. Both 0.3 and 0.5 results are retained.

    Both winners are frozen before generating any final observations. Final tests compare the preceding primary vanilla recipe with the selected recipe on five fresh trajectories per system, three fresh seeds and three starting points. Each window spans ten Lyapunov times. Results at that limit are right-censored: they establish accuracy through the window, without estimating its later end. The independent unit is a trajectory, not each nested forecast.

    Reproduce the experiment¶

    Install the repository's development, HPO and dynamics extras, and the pinned benchmark requirements, in an isolated environment. From the repository root:

    python benchmarks/vanilla_robustness.py --output /tmp/resdag-vanilla-study

    The output directory must not exist. --quick runs a small execution smoke; its short synthetic protocol is not forecast-quality evidence. --trials and --timeout allow explicit budgets; changing them defines a different experiment.

    The runner freezes the runtime source and records source/helper hashes, dependency versions, IC and reservoir seed banks, generated observations, warning messages, completed and failed trials, controlled reservoir fingerprints, frozen winners, full error traces and prediction hashes. Partial final failures retain a failure receipt. Dataset hashes are checked before loading. Its saved runner.py is a provenance copy: rerun it from the original repository layout with the recorded runtime, helpers and dependencies. It is not a standalone installed application.

    Timing observations in this study are exploratory. They do not establish a controlled CPU ranking or any CUDA performance claim. See the phase-five measurements for the separate speed evidence.

    Held-out results¶

    The run completed all 48 validation trials: 24 per system, with 432 fits and 1,296 forecasts. Both selected recipes were frozen before the final bank was generated. Final evaluation used 60 separate fits and 180 forecasts, including each incumbent control, with no exclusions or failed fits.

    The main statistic is the median of five independent trajectory medians. Each trajectory contributes nine forecasts from three initialization seeds and three starting points. The individual range shows the nested forecasts' spread; it is not a confidence interval. A ≥ sign means the forecast remained below the threshold through the ten-Lyapunov-time observation window.

    System / recipe Median at 0.3 (LT) Median at 0.5 (LT) Individual range at 0.3 (LT) Censored at 0.3
    Lorenz incumbent and selected recipe ≥10.016 ≥10.016 2.155–≥10.016 27 / 45
    Rössler incumbent 3.013 3.810 0.849–9.225 0 / 45
    Rössler selected recipe 6.750 7.154 1.677–≥10.001 1 / 45

    All five trajectory medians at the main 0.3 threshold, in their recorded order:

    • Lorenz: [≥10.016, 5.995, ≥10.016, 9.654, ≥10.016].
    • Rössler incumbent: [3.013, 2.899, 3.810, 3.800, 2.485].
    • Rössler selected: [7.144, 6.253, 6.750, 8.779, 6.233].

    Lorenz retained the existing primary recipe. Rössler improved from 3.01 to 6.75 LT on the fresh test bank, but the typical 7–8 LT goal at 0.3 remains unmet. The more forgiving 0.5 result does not replace that criterion. These are bounded vanilla-model accuracy results, not a comparison against other libraries or a guarantee for other systems.

    Both winners use input_to_readout=True. In the controlled validation pair, Lorenz scored 10.016 LT with the input and 4.800 LT without it; Rössler scored 4.576 and 4.638 LT respectively. All other settings and initialized reservoirs were identical within each pair. The later Rössler gain comes from selecting a whole recipe; it cannot be attributed to the new switch alone. The default therefore remains unchanged, and tuning can still select either configuration.

    The selected Rössler recipe is:

    import torch
import resdag as rd

model = rd.ESN(
    input_size=3,
    reservoir_size=400,
    spectral_radius=0.316473400694296,
    leak_rate=0.6988018952693786,
    feedback_initializer=("random", {"input_scaling": 0.3162866539497749}),
    bias_scaling=0.029171927900014934,
    alpha=2.8843872485074098e-11,
    topology=("erdos_renyi", {"p": 6 / 400, "directed": True, "self_loops": True}),
    washout=500,
    seed=7849201,
    input_to_readout=True,
    readout="svd",
    dtype=torch.float64,
)

    Use the recorded protocol's training length, washout, sampling interval and training-only scaling when reproducing these measurements. A parameter list alone does not reproduce an experiment; the download below records the full configuration and exact source.

    Failures and integrity¶

    All validation fits completed, but 104 of the 648 Rössler validation forecasts became nonfinite. They remain in the report and are scored at their first invalid error, rather than being discarded. Lorenz validation and all 180 final forecasts were finite. No dataset-integrity failure or fit failure was observed. These are different conditions: a completed fit can still produce an unstable autonomous forecast.

    After the API and persistence cleanup, a separate replay refitted all 60 final models. All 180 complete prediction arrays matched their recorded SHA-256 hashes byte for byte on this host. An independent NumPy calculation recovered every threshold crossing and error curve. This checks the adopted cleanup against the recorded experiment; it does not establish cross-device bitwise reproducibility.

    Download the complete protocol, source manifest, frozen winners, every trial, error trace and prediction hash. The forecasting and animation guide uses the first predeclared held-out case for each system. Those display cases are fixed, include the complete recorded window and are not the headline aggregate.

    Verification status¶

    The scientific evaluation and exact replay are complete. The release-readiness record in the roadmap distinguishes local, installed-package and hosted verification. Phase five remains open for typical Rössler accuracy, comparative CPU speed and broader model/hardware scales.
